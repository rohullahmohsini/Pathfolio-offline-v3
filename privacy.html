Pathfolio: Privacy Policy

Privacy first. This application is designed to work offline and keep user information on the user’s own device.

What the app collects

Only what the student types or uploads: name, student ID, class, and optionally school, city or province, email, phone, links, an ID document link, a profile photo, and the CV or portfolio content (education, projects, achievements, images and links).

Where it is stored

Everything is stored only in the browser or application storage on the student’s own device. The application does not send this information to a server. The student can delete it at any time with the “Clear” buttons.

How it is shared

Nothing is shared unless the student chooses to share it. The PDF is created on the student’s own device. The user then chooses which installed application or service to use for sharing.

Children and young people

Students under 18 may share their portfolio only with the agreement of a parent or guardian. The app asks the student to confirm this before the sharing options are enabled.

Third-party code

Pathfolio does not load third-party scripts, fonts or analytics. Links that the student adds (for example Google Drive or YouTube) open the student’s own pages on those services.

Your choices

You can delete your locally stored information at any time. Uninstalling the application also removes its application storage.
